# Two Lines to Your Past Self

Entry for [make.fun's "Two Lines to Your Past Self" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca): a two-line warning sent back to the writer's past self.

> Don't tell your mother you are too busy to come home for her sixtieth
> birthday in March.
> She will keep your plate warm for three hours, and the empty chair will
> be the last thing you picture whenever anyone says family dinner.

`main.py` writes exactly those two lines (226 characters) to
`/output/entry.txt`, after checking the competition gate: two lines, one
sentence each, 1 to 280 characters, not only whitespace. No network, no
input files, no arguments.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
